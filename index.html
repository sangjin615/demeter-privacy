Summary — Demeter does not ask for an account, your name, your email, or anything else that identifies you. Settings and play logs stay on your PC. Your survey answers and anonymous play logs are sent to the developers only when you choose to submit the in-game survey.

1. What we process and how

a. Data stored only on your PC (never sent)

DataLocation
Settings (volume, mute, fullscreen, playback speed, language) and progress (Golden Seeds, shop upgrade levels, tutorial completion, selected difficulty)Windows registry HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Zinius Games\Demeter

b. Data sent only when you submit the survey

The data below is sent only when you press Submit in the lobby's survey. Submitting is optional, and the game works the same if you don't.

  • Your survey answers (multiple choice and free text)
  • The anonymous play logs from (a) that have not been sent before
  • The random session ID

Please don't write personal details such as your name or contact information in the free-text answers. If you do, we won't use them for any other purpose and will delete them on request.

c. Data generated automatically

When the survey is sent, your IP address may reach Google's servers as part of the connection. We do not receive or store IP addresses. We do not use Unity's analytics, ads, crash reporting, or hardware statistics services.

2. Why we use it

Only to balance difficulty, find and fix bugs, and improve the tutorial. Never for advertising, marketing, or profiling.

3. Retention and deletion

  • Survey data: deleted promptly one year after collection, or earlier on request.
  • Data on your PC: you can delete it yourself (see section 6).
  • Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered.

4. Service providers and international transfer

We use the service below to receive and store survey data, which means the data is transferred outside your country.

If you don't want this transfer, simply don't submit the survey. We don't share your data with anyone else.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit with HTTPS, and access to the Google account and documents that store survey data is limited to the operator.

6. Your rights

  • Access or delete submitted survey data: email us with the date and approximate time you submitted and any answers you remember. Because we don't collect names or other identifiers, we need these details to find your data.
  • Delete data on your PC: delete the folder and registry key listed in section 1. This removes all settings, progress, and play logs. Uninstalling the game may leave this data in place.

7. Children

Demeter does not collect names, contact information, or other data that identifies children. We ask parents to make sure children don't write personal details in the survey's free-text answers.
